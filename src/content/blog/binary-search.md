Binary search is one of the algorithms that has a very obvious intuition, and you think you understand it, until you start writing the code. There are simply so many variants of binary search with different boundary conditions, pointer update rules, and so on. So I decided to put together everything that I know for binary search over the many LeetCode questions I did.

**FYI, these are the questions:**
- LC 981, [Time-based Key Value Store](https://leetcode.com/problems/time-based-key-value-store)
- LC 33, [Search in a Rotated Sorted Array](https://leetcode.com/problems/search-in-rotated-sorted-array)
- LC 1658, [Minimum Operations to Reduce X to Zero](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-operations-to-reduce-x-to-zero)
- LC 713, [Subarray Product Less Than K](https://leetcode.com/problems/subarray-product-less-than-k)
- LC 74, [Search a 2D Matrix](https://leetcode.com/problems/search-a-2d-matrix/)
- LC 4, [Median of Two Sorted Arrays](https://leetcode.com/problems/median-of-two-sorted-arrays)
